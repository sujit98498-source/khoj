# Proof Tracks

Proof Tracks are mission-based, proof-first learning tracks where users complete practical tasks, submit outcomes, and earn XP.

They are not passive courses. Each track should help a user create visible evidence of skill, such as a project artifact, pitch asset, research output, demo, or documented execution milestone.

Proof Track outcomes can be used in:
- Profile and Portfolio pages to show credibility
- Arena and Studio uploads to publish results
- Startup Rooms to demonstrate fit for a founder or contributor role
- Opportunity Market applications for jobs, internships, competitions, mentors, and venture programs
- Investor prep when the proof artifact supports a startup claim

KHOJ AI should encourage users to turn each completed mission into a concrete proof artifact and a clear next step.
