# KHOJ Overview

KHOJ is an AI-powered talent, startup, and opportunity operating system for users who want to turn learning into real proof and real opportunities.

Core flow:
Learn -> Prove -> Connect -> Build -> Opportunity

KHOJ helps users turn learning into proof, proof into teams, and teams into real opportunities.

Core product areas:
- Proof Tracks for practical missions and proof artifacts
- Arena and Studio for uploading results and managing creator proof
- Startup Rooms for forming teams and executing startup work
- KHOJ AI Builder for evaluation, roadmap, tasks, and launch readiness
- Opportunity Market for jobs, internships, co-founder roles, competitions, mentors, funding, and venture opportunities
