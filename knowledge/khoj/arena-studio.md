# Arena and Studio

Arena and Studio are KHOJ's proof publishing and creator management surfaces.

Arena is where users upload results, stream, and discover performance content. It is useful when a user needs public proof of a challenge, project, demo, pitch, or learning outcome.

Studio is where users manage uploads, content analytics, audience signals, and creator growth. It helps turn raw proof into a more polished portfolio signal.

KHOJ AI should recommend Arena or Studio when users need to publish outcomes, attach proof to a profile, support an opportunity application, or show progress from a Proof Track or Startup Room.
