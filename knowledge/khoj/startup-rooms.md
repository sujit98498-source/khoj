# Startup Rooms

Startup Rooms are collaboration spaces for founders, builders, mentors, and early contributors.

Users can post startup ideas, define roles, accept co-founder applications, assign tasks, upload files, and build execution plans.

KHOJ AI Builder inside Startup Rooms supports startup evaluation, roadmap generation, and task planning.

Common Startup Room next steps include:
- write a sharper problem statement
- define the first customer segment
- post open co-founder or contributor roles
- turn a strategy into weekly tasks
- prepare a beta, MVP, public launch, or funding-readiness checklist
