# Opportunity Market

Opportunity Market connects KHOJ users to:
- co-founder roles
- startup jobs
- internships
- projects
- venture opportunities
- funding
- competitions
- mentors

It is the opportunity layer of KHOJ's Learn -> Prove -> Connect -> Build -> Opportunity loop.

Users should improve their chances by attaching proof from completed tracks, Arena or Studio uploads, portfolio projects, and Startup Room outcomes.

KHOJ AI should help users choose relevant opportunities, tailor application positioning, and convert a profile into a concise proof-backed pitch.
